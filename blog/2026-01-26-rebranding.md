# Scriptura: A New Name, New Home, and What’s Coming Next

I'm excited to share meaningful updates to Scriptura, the Discord Bible bot many of you have been using and supporting. What started as a small personal project has grown into something I'm genuinely thankful for—and with that growth comes some important improvements and features.

<!-- truncate -->

---

## About Scriptura

Scriptura is designed with a clear mission: making Scripture accessible, shareable, and central to our conversations. The name emphasizes the Word itself rather than the tool, reflecting the heart of this project. It's built to make studying and sharing the Bible simple and intuitive for everyone.

---

## A New Website and Documentation

Along with the rename, Scriptura now has a dedicated website and documentation hub:

👉 **https://scriptura.prinke.dev**

The new site includes:
- Clear getting-started guides
- Command documentation
- Privacy and terms pages
- Self-hosting instructions
- Ongoing updates as new features are added

My goal with the docs is to make Scriptura easy to use for everyone—whether you’re just adding it to a server or looking to run your own instance.

---

## Expanded Bible Translation Support

Scriptura now supports more Bible translations than before, giving users greater flexibility when reading and sharing Scripture.

Currently supported translations include (and this list will continue to grow):

- ESV
- NIV
- NLT
- CSB
- KJV
- NKJV
- And many more (also in other langauges!)

If you don’t specify a translation, Scriptura defaults to **ESV**. You can also save a preferred translation so you don’t have to specify it every time.

---

## What’s Coming Next

Scriptura is still actively being developed, and there’s more on the way. Some upcoming features and ideas include:

- Additional Bible translations
- Enhanced daily verse functionality
- Audio Bibles in voice channels
- Additional Bible study tools

As always, feedback and suggestions are welcome. Scriptura is open source, and I’m grateful for everyone who contributes, reports issues, or simply uses the bot to share God’s Word.
