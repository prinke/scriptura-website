# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: January 24, 2026_

Scriptura ("the bot", "we", "our", or "us") is a Discord bot that allows users to retrieve Bible scripture using slash commands. Your privacy is important to us, and this policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and what we do **not** collect.

---

## Information We Collect

Scriptura is designed to collect the **absolute minimum amount of data** required to function.

### Stored User Data

We store **only one optional piece of user data**:

- A user’s preferred Bible translation (if the user chooses to set one)

This data is used solely to provide a better user experience and is not shared with third parties.

---

## Information We Do Not Collect

Scriptura does **not** collect or store:

- Message content
- Scripture requests or verse history
- Server names or server metadata
- Usernames or discriminator tags
- Analytics or behavioral tracking data
- IP addresses
- Location data

We do not build user profiles or track usage patterns.

---

## How Scripture Is Retrieved

When you use commands such as `/verse` or `/daily-verse`, Scriptura retrieves scripture text from third-party Bible APIs.

Currently used APIs include:

- **ESV.org API**
- **api.bible**

Your verse request is sent to these services **only for the purpose of retrieving scripture text**. Scriptura does not attach personal identifiers beyond what is technically required to fulfill the request.

Please note that these services operate under their own privacy policies.

---

## How We Use Your Data

Any stored data (such as a preferred translation) is used only to:

- Determine which Bible translation to return by default
- Improve command usability

We do not sell, rent, or monetize user data.

---

## Data Retention

- Preferred translation data is retained until:
  - You change it
  - You remove the bot
  - The data is manually deleted as part of maintenance

We do not retain historical records of scripture usage.

---

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect stored data from unauthorized access. However, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

---

## Children’s Privacy

Scriptura is not designed to knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly store personal data of minors.

---

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date.

---

## Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the project’s public repository.
